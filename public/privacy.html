Privacy Policy – CoffieCalm

Effective Date: 10 February 2026

Information We Collect

When you use CoffieCalm, we collect the following information:

  • Email address and username
  • Hashed passwords (encrypted for security)
  • Messages and chat history
  • Friend information and connections
  • Mood logs and tracking data
  • Technical usage data (device info, IP address)

How We Use Data

We use your information to:

  • Provide messaging and chat features
  • Store and display mood tracking information
  • Maintain platform security and prevent abuse
  • Improve the app experience and functionality
  • Communicate important updates to users

Data Storage

All data is stored securely using Firebase services, which employ industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal data to third parties. Your information is used solely to provide and improve CoffieCalm services.

Security

We take security seriously. All passwords are encrypted using industry-standard hashing algorithms. We follow best practices to protect your data, though no system can guarantee 100% security.

User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request account deletion at any time
  • Export your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@coffiecalm.com

CRISIS AND MENTAL HEALTH DISCLAIMER

IMPORTANT NOTICE:

CoffieCalm is NOT an emergency service or suicide prevention platform.

User messages, chats, and mood logs are NOT monitored for suicide risk or self-harm behavior. We do not analyze or track users for crisis situations.

If you are experiencing suicidal thoughts, self-harm intentions, or any emotional crisis, you must contact emergency services immediately.
